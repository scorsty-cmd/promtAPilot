If You Were Spartacus's Best Friend (Gladiator Life & the Slave War, 73–71 BC)

DESCRIPTION
You're a gladiator in Capua in 73 BC — and your cellmate is Spartacus. Live the whole story at his side: the oath, the training post, barley porridge, your first fight in the arena, the breakout with kitchen knives, the vine ladders down Vesuvius, an army of 70,000, the split with Crixus, Crassus's wall, and the last morning.
