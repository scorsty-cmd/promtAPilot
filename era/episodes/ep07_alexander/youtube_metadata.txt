TITLE
If You Were Alexander the Great's Classmate (Taught by Aristotle, 343 BC)

DESCRIPTION
You sat next to Alexander in Aristotle's classroom at Mieza. Twenty years later you're at the feast in Samarkand where he kills his friend Cleitus. Live the whole story at his side: Bucephalus, the golden mean, Philip's murder, Diogenes, the Granicus, the Gordian knot, Issus, Gaugamela, Persepolis in flames, Porus and the war elephants, the mutiny, the desert, and Babylon.
