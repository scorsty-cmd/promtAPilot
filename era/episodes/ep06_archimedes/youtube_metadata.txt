DESCRIPTION
Syracuse, 212 BC. The Romans are inside the city, and an old man is still drawing circles in the sand.
Go back twenty years: you're the boy who delivers his bread — and becomes his apprentice. You're there for "Eureka!", for the day one old man launches the biggest ship ever built, for counting the grains of sand in the universe, for trapping π — and for the two years when his machines held off the Roman navy.

Every key moment is marked MYTH, EVIDENCE or MAYBE. You and Kleon are fictional; Archimedes, Hiero II, Marcellus and Cicero are real, and the events come from Polybius, Livy, Plutarch, Vitruvius and Cicero. Some dialogue is dramatized.
