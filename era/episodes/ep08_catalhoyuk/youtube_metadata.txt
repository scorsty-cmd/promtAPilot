You Were Born in a Town With No Doors (Çatalhöyük, 7000 BC)
Life in the World's Strangest Stone Age Town — Çatalhöyük

DESCRIPTION
There are no streets in your town. No doors. No windows. You climb a ladder through the roof to go outside — and your grandmother is buried under the platform where you sleep.
Grow up in Çatalhöyük, one of the biggest towns on Earth 9,000 years ago, on the Konya plain in today's Turkey: white plastered walls and red handprints, bull horns in the walls, streets in the sky, an aurochs hunt, obsidian mirrors, a volcano on the horizon, smoke in your lungs, a storyteller buried under the floor, and a whole town that grew into a hill, house on house.
