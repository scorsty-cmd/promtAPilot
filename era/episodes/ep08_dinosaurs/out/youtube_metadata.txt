DESCRIPTION
66 million years ago, a rock 10 km wide hit Mexico. In this video, it misses.
You wake up in a greenhouse world with no ice caps, guided by Trood — a talking descendant of the troodonts. You meet the great-great-grandchild of a tyrannosaur, try not to swim with a mosasaur, find out where the mammals went (spoiler: they stayed tiny), visit a town of woven nests, and run from raptors at night. Then you wake up… and the dinosaurs were outside your window all along.

Key claims are marked EVIDENCE, MAYBE or MYTH. Trood, Pip and the "Earth B" world are speculative fiction; the science around them is real and sourced below.
