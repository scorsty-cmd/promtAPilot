DESCRIPTION
The Greeks are gone. They left a giant wooden horse. Your friends are celebrating. In six hours, your city will burn.
You are an ordinary Trojan soldier. From the day Paris brings Helen home, through ten years of siege, the battle at the ships, Hector's last goodbye, King Priam in Achilles' tent, and the night of the horse — and then the real question: was any of it true?

Every key moment is marked MYTH, EVIDENCE or MAYBE. You and your friend Ilos are fictional; the story follows Homer's Iliad, the Odyssey and Virgil's Aeneid, and the archaeology of Hisarlık (Troy) near Çanakkale, Türkiye.
