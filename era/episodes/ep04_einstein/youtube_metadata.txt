DESCRIPTION
Bern, 1902. A 23-year-old with no job puts a tiny ad in the newspaper: private lessons in mathematics and physics, trial lesson free.
You answer it — and for the next fifty years you're there for everything: the Olympia Academy dinners, the 1905 miracle year, the happiest thought of his life, the eclipse that made him famous overnight, the great argument with Niels Bohr, exile, the letter to Roosevelt, and the last night in Princeton.

You're a fictional companion; the events, places and most of the quoted lines are documented. Some dialogue is dramatized.
