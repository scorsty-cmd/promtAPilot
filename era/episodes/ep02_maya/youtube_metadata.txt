THUMBNAIL TEXT: BORN MAYA?  ·  AD 750

DESCRIPTION
What if you were born in a Maya city 1,300 years ago? Your money might be chocolate, your head might be shaped by a cradleboard, and your name might come from a 260-day sacred calendar.

In this animated story you live a whole life in Late Classic Tikal (c. AD 750): your family compound, maize fields, the ballgame, the fear of Xibalba, the astronomers and kings, the great drought, and the twist most people get wrong: the Maya never disappeared.
