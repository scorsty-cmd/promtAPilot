STEP 3 — Build the vertical Short (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, yuv420p, -movflags +faststart) with ffmpeg:

Foreground: the footage scaled to 1080 px wide (or a slightly zoomed center crop up to ~1.25x if the key action stays in frame), placed slightly above center. Check visually that maps, labels and portraits in the segment stay readable; if an important label is cut off, use less zoom.
- Hook text from frame 0: a big, bold, high-contrast line (max ~7 words) in the top area, visible for the first ~3 seconds, true to the facts in the video.
- Large burned-in captions for every spoken line, re-timed from the SRT to the cut, max ~2 lines, in the lower-middle safe zone (not covered by YouTube's UI at the bottom ~350 px or right edge).
- Last ~2 seconds: "Full story on History Sourced" end text over the footage. Keep the original narration, music and sound effects; fade audio in/out over ~0.15 s at each cut; make sure peak volume (volumedetect max) is below -0.5 dB.
- Apply today's planned improvement(s).
- Everything must stay the channel's own original footage; add no third-party images, clips, music or fonts beyond what ffmpeg/the system provides or the toolkit fonts.
