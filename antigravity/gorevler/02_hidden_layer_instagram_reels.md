REEL FORMAT RULES (set by the user on 17 Oct 2026 after the first Reel had an 80% skip rate and 13% average watch; these OVERRIDE REELS_PLAYBOOK.md wherever they conflict):
- Length 20–30 seconds total (hard limit 32 s). One single idea: hook → 2–4 punchy fact/receipt beats → payoff (what the viewer can check or do) → save CTA. Roughly 55–75 narrated words.
- Frame 0 (before any narration): a shocking one-line money statement or striking number in HUGE text (≥40% of frame width, max ~7 words) on a high-contrast background, already moving from frame 0 — no logo intro, no fade-in from black. (reel2.py's `hook` beat does this.)
- Something changes on screen at least every 1.5–2 seconds.
- Large burned-in captions for every spoken line, in the safe zone (reel2.py handles placement), styled per the hidden-layer-style skill.
- End with an on-screen and spoken save CTA ("Save this before you forget" / "Save this — you'll need it") plus "Follow for the hidden rules" as a small second line. Put the same save CTA in the caption's first or last line.
- Caption: first line repeats the hook (≤125 characters), then 1–2 short lines, sources line, photo/footage credit lines for any CC BY photo or B-roll, "Not financial advice." when relevant, and 3–5 relevant hashtags.
- The hook must be TRUE and backed by today's sources; shocking ≠ exaggerated. No fear-mongering or clickbait.
- Before publishing, check: duration 20–32 s with ffprobe, frame 0 shows the big hook text (extract and LOOK at it), captions readable, fonts are the skill's fonts (not a fallback), CTA present at the end, volumedetect max < -0.5 dB, and look at at least 6 frames. The MP4 must be vertical 1080x1920 (needed for YouTube Shorts too). If any check fails, fix and rebuild.
