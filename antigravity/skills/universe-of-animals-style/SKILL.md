# Universe Of Animals — Style DNA

Concept: **"the animal's own commentary."** The joke comes from what the animal is (supposedly) thinking. Captions look like hand-placed stickers, warm and playful, never the default white-text-black-outline TikTok look. All on-screen text and voiceover in Turkish.

Use for every Universe Of Animals Short, the animal Instagram page Reels/stories, and posts promoting the DijitalStore e-books.

## Font (free, OFL, full Turkish support — ş ğ ı İ ç ö ü verified)
From `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/`:
- `ofl/bricolagegrotesque/BricolageGrotesque[opsz,wdth,wght].ttf` (URL-encode brackets). Captions: weight 800, width ~85. Labels: weight 700.

Always test Turkish characters after loading; if any glyph is missing, stop and fix the font, never ship tofu boxes.

## Layout (1080×1920)
- Video clip fills the frame.
- Top-left: dark cocoa pill "iç ses: …" (the animal's inner voice), cream text, sky dot at the end.
- Captions as stickers in the lower-middle third (y ≈ 1150–1500), never covering the animal's face.
- Sticker = rounded rect (radius 36), cream or tangerine fill, 8px cocoa outline, solid cocoa offset shadow (10px right/down), rotated −5° to +5°. Setup line on cream, punchline on tangerine.

## Signature moves
1. **Sticker slap** — each sticker pops in: scale 0.6 → 1.08 → 1.0 over 6 frames with a soft "pop" sound.
2. **İç ses** — the inner-voice pill appears exactly on the animal's reaction moment.
3. **Freeze-frame punch** — on the funniest frame, freeze 0.4–0.6 s, slight 4% zoom, punchline sticker lands.
4. **Paw counter** — for list Shorts ("3 sebep…"), a small cocoa circle with the number.

## Editing
- Hook in the first 1 s: the strangest frame first, then rewind to the setup.
- 15–35 s total. Cut dead air hard. One freeze-frame punch per Short max.
- Turkish voiceover: conversational, comic timing, pauses before punchlines. Vary voice tone/character per animal; don't use the same flat delivery every day.
- E-book promotion: one cream sticker at the end ("Kedinizi daha iyi anlamak için: link profilde"), never mid-video, not on every single Short (roughly 1 in 3).

## Content & monetization safety
- Only clips with a licence that allows reuse; keep the source/licence noted per clip.
- Never use animals in distress, injury, fear or "rescue" setups — YouTube's July 2026 policy removes channels built on distressing content from monetization.
- Add real value beyond the clip: the Turkish commentary/inner-voice joke and edit must transform the footage, so it is not a plain re-upload.
- Vary format daily (inner-voice joke / "3 sebep" mini-fact / before-after / reaction compilation) and log which was used so no format repeats 3 days in a row.

## Before delivering
Render a frame at the punchline moment and check: Turkish characters render, stickers don't cover the face, text readable at phone size.
