Your ChatGPT chats aren't just yours. Save this before you forget.

OpenAI's help page says that on Free, Plus and Pro, it "may use your content to train our models." To opt out: Settings > Data controls > turn off "Improve the model for everyone" (or use OpenAI's Privacy Portal). Temporary Chats aren't used for training, but OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety.
