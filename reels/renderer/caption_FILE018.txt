Same groceries, same store, prices up to 23% apart. Save this before you forget.

In a Consumer Reports/Groundwork Collaborative test, 437 shoppers added identical items to carts; almost 75% of items showed more than one price, with up to a 23% gap on a single item. Instacart called the tests limited, short-term and randomized and says it doesn't use personal demographic data. In August 2026 the FTC proposed a policy statement: businesses that don't tell consumers how personal data is used to set a price may violate the FTC Act. It's a proposal, not final. The carts in the Reel are example numbers.
