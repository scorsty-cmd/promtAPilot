Pay in dollars abroad? A card machine can add up to 12% extra. Save this before you forget.

When a foreign terminal asks to charge you in your own currency (dynamic currency conversion), the rate usually carries a markup. A German consumer test found 2.6% to 12.0% higher prices when it was used. Visa's own page says the choice is yours.

Sources: Stiftung Warentest via Motley Fool (15 May 2023); Visa, "Dynamic currency conversion" page. Read 6 Nov 2026. Example numbers on screen are illustrations. Not financial advice.
