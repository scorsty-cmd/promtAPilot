Advertisers know your phone by a number. Save this before you forget.
Android gives every phone an advertising ID. Apps and advertisers can use it to recognize your device.
Google's own page: "The advertising ID is a unique, user-resettable, and user-deletable ID for advertising, provided by Google Play services."
Delete it: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID. It won't end all tracking, but it cuts one big link.
Sources: Google Play Console Help "Advertising ID" (support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6048248, read 24 Oct 2026) · ghacks.net "How to delete the Advertising ID on Android"
