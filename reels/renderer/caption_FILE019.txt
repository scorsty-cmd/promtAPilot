Hard to cancel? In New York City, that now costs the company $525 or more. Save this before you forget.

NYC's click-to-cancel rule has been in effect since 1 Oct 2026: if you signed up for a subscription online, you should be able to cancel online. Fines start at $525 for a first violation, $1,050 for a second and $3,500 for a third or later, per violation. Businesses can also owe restitution for charges after your first cancel attempt. This rule applies in New York City only. The fine amounts in the Reel are the city's published schedule.

Sources: NYC Mayor's Office release, 1 Oct 2026 (nyc.gov); Inside Privacy on the NYC rule (penalties, § 20-703). Read 3 Nov 2026. Not financial or legal advice.
