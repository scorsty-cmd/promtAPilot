Did your car raise your insurance? Save this before you forget.

The FTC says GM tracked where and how people drove, sometimes every 3 seconds, and sold data like hard braking, late-night driving and speeding to consumer reporting agencies. Insurers used those reports to set rates and deny coverage. GM ended its Smart Driver program in April 2024, and a final FTC order (Jan 14, 2026) bans it from sharing that data with consumer reporting agencies for 5 years.

Drive a connected car? Open its app and check the data-sharing settings.
