Pay in 4? 63% of users stack loans. Save this before you forget.

The CFPB found about 63% of Pay-in-4 borrowers had overlapping loans at some point in a year, and 10.5% of users were charged a late fee in 2021. Three small purchases can mean a payment due every two weeks (example in the Reel: 3 x $100 = $75 per date).

Sources: CFPB press release, 13 Jan 2025 (2022 data, six lenders); CFPB report, 15 Sep 2022 (2021 data, five lenders). Read 4 Nov 2026. Not financial advice.
