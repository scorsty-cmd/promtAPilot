TITLE:
Same Eggs, 5 Prices: How Apps Decide What You Pay
DESCRIPTION:
Same eggs, same store, same app: shoppers in one test saw five different prices, from $3.99 to $4.79. Here's what surveillance pricing really is.
You'll learn what's documented and what's a myth, what the FTC's August 2026 proposal says sellers must disclose, and 4 checks to see whether the price you're shown is the price.

What's inside: the Instacart price-test study (437 volunteers, 74% of items with more than one price, ~$1,200/year projected gap), what data pricing tools can use according to the FTC's 2025 staff findings, four claims checked (Mac users, low battery, store apps, airline AI), the FTC's proposed personalized-pricing policy (19 Aug 2026), New York's disclosure law and 2026 state laws.
